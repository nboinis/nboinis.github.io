**Period:** 2023 – 2024

The breach of the Kakhovka dam during the Russo-Ukrainian war caused an environmental disaster in the Dnieper River basin. In this project, we applied wide-scope target and non-target analysis to assess organic contaminants in **surface water, groundwater and sediments** from the basin.

This work formed the basis of my MSc thesis and was presented at SETAC Europe 2024 in Seville.
