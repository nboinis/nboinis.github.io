**Period:** 2022 – 2023 and 2024 – 2025  
**Partner:** Landesanstalt für Umwelt Baden-Württemberg (LUBW)

Biomonitoring research on biota samples from Germany, including terrestrial mesocarnivores. HRMS-based workflows were used to reveal which organic micropollutants occur in different wildlife species.

**Related output:** [CEST2023 poster: Best Poster Presentation Award](/talks/2023-cest2023-mesocarnivores).
