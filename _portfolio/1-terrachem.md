**Period:** 2024 – present  
**Project website:** [terrachem.eu](https://terrachem.eu/)

[TerraChem](https://terrachem.eu/) is a European research project on the chemical burden of terrestrial ecosystems. Within the TrAMS group, I work mainly in **Work Package 1**, analysing samples that represent both abiotic and biotic components of European terrestrial ecosystems.

![Graphical abstract: multi-platform MS approach for organic micropollutants in terrestrial invertebrates](https://media.springernature.com/lw685/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00216-026-06882-8/MediaObjects/216_2026_6882_Figa_HTML.png)
*Graphical abstract from Boinis et al. (2026), Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry, [doi:10.1007/s00216-026-06882-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-026-06882-8). Reproduced unmodified under [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).*

My role includes:
* developing and applying sample preparation protocols for complex environmental matrices such as soil and biota
* wide-scope target and non-target analysis using GC-HRMS, LC-HRMS and LC-TIMS-HRMS
* chemometric data processing and quality control of low- and high-resolution MS results
* developing GC-HRMS methods, including GC-APCI-QToF MS
