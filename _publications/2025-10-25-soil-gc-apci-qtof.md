Three sample preparation approaches (modified QuEChERS, accelerated solvent extraction and ultrasound-assisted extraction) were developed and compared for the broad-scope analysis of organic micropollutants in soil by GC-APCI-QToF MS. The modified QuEChERS method was selected and fully validated for 75 compounds, including pesticides, PAHs, PCBs, PCNs and organochlorine pesticides, and applied to six soil samples from Greece, where 31 analytes were detected at trace levels.

### In plain words

Soil can hold a mixture of many different pollutants, yet most studies look for only a few chemical families at a time. We developed and compared three ways of extracting pollutants from soil, so that a single analysis on a high-resolution mass spectrometer can cover many families at once: pesticides, PAHs, PCBs and other chlorinated industrial compounds. The best of the three was fully validated and then tested on soils from Greece, where the results hint that pollutant levels change over time, a good reason for wider monitoring.
