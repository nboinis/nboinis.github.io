Measuring organic micropollutants in the livers of apex species is a powerful biomonitoring tool, revealing environmental pollution and chemical exposure at higher trophic levels. This critical review surveys recent extraction approaches for liver tissue (ultrasound-assisted extraction, pressurised liquid extraction, Soxhlet and QuEChERS) and compares them in terms of time, robustness, cost and environmental impact.
