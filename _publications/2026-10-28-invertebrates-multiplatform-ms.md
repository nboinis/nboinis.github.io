Terrestrial invertebrates play key roles in ecosystems and accumulate organic micropollutants from agricultural, urban and industrial sources, yet existing biomonitoring methods usually target narrow, class-specific chemical spaces. This open-access paper presents a multi-platform mass spectrometry approach for the broad-scope determination of organic micropollutants in terrestrial invertebrates.

### In plain words

Small land animals such as bees and snails pick up pollutants from farms, towns and industry, but most monitoring methods look for only one chemical family at a time. We developed ways to prepare these animals for analysis on three kinds of mass spectrometer, so that one sample can be checked for pesticides, pharmaceuticals, PFAS, PAHs, PCBs and more. Tested on honeybees and land snails from Greece, the approach found long-known legacy pollutants, pesticides in current use and industrial chemicals in the same samples.
