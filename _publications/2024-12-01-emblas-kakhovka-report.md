Chapter of the EU4EMBLAS technical report on the impact of the destruction of the Nova Kakhovka dam (6 June 2023) on the Dnipro River and the Black Sea. Water and sediment samples were analysed by wide-scope target and suspect screening for organic micropollutants and metals, and the results were provisionally stored in the NORMAN Database System.
