Poster 3.24.P-Th097

**Boinis N.**, Dragazis N., Panagopoulou E., Gkotsis G., Thomaidis N.S.

**Summary:** Three sample preparation methods (accelerated solvent extraction, ultrasound-assisted extraction and a modified QuEChERS) were compared for broad-scope analysis of organic micropollutants in soil by GC-APCI-QToF MS. The modified QuEChERS method performed best and was validated for 75 legacy and emerging pollutants, including PAHs, PCBs, PCNs and organochlorine pesticides. Applied to soils from Attica, Greece, it detected 31 compounds at ultra-trace levels.
