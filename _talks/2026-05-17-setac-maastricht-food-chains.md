**Boinis N.**, Konomi A., Karageorgoudi A., Preza Mayo Kataki D., Kontogianni P., Dragazis N., Mourtzoukou F.A., Floros N., Panagopoulou E., Aalizadeh R., Alygizakis N., et al.

**Summary:** Within the EU project [TerraChem](https://terrachem.eu/), soil, plants, invertebrates, rodents, prey species and apex predators, such as red foxes, grey wolves, European badgers and hedgehogs, were sampled in six European countries in 2024–2025. The samples were analysed by LC-ion mobility-HRMS, GC-HRMS and GC-MS/MS for target and non-target screening. Hundreds of organic micropollutants were found, including PFAS concentrated in apex predator livers, legacy pesticides such as DDT, and anticoagulant rodenticides.
