Poster 3.04.P-Th199

**Boinis N.**, Gkotsis G., Diamanti K., Nika M.-C., Alygizakis N., Slobodnik J., Thomaidis N.S.

**Summary:** Surface water and sediment samples collected from the Dnieper River in Ukraine in 2023 were screened with HRMS-based target and suspect screening workflows. Preliminary results revealed industrial chemicals, pharmaceuticals, stimulants and pesticides, and the findings were compared with an earlier sampling campaign from 2020. The abstract is published in the [SETAC Europe 2024 abstract book](https://www.setac.org/resource/seville-2024-abstract-book-pdf.html).
